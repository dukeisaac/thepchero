G.T. Window Tinting & Auto Glass LLC is a trusted local provider of professional window tinting and auto glass services proudly serving Chicago for more than 28 years. Our skilled technicians deliver precise, high-quality results for every project, whether you want to enhance your vehicle’s comfort, improve your home’s energy efficiency, or upgrade your business with premium solar control films. We offer a full range of tinting options including ceramic, carbon, dyed, and mirror films, each designed to reduce heat, block harmful UV rays, and increase privacy while maintaining a clean and stylish look.

In addition to window tinting, we provide expert auto glass replacement and repair for windshields, door glass, sunroofs, window regulators, and side mirrors. Every job is completed using reliable materials and careful attention to detail, ensuring lasting performance and customer satisfaction. At G.T. Window Tinting & Auto Glass, you can count on efficient, affordable, and friendly service from a bilingual team that values honesty and professionalism. Whether you need tinting or glass work for your car, truck, home, or commercial space, we make sure every project is handled with care and done right the first time.
